ABOUT US

About Campus Lost & Found

Helping students get their belongings back.

Our mission

Losing a phone, a book or an ID card on campus is stressful, and finding someone else's belongings can leave you wondering what to do with them. Campus Lost & Found gives every student one simple place to report what they lost and what they found, so belongings can find their way back to their owners.

How it works

1

Report

Tell us whether you lost or found something, where and when, and describe it in a few words.

2

Match

When a found report matches a lost one, the lost report is removed automatically and the found item's details are shown.

3

Reunite

The owner recognises the item from its details and gets it back. Owners can also mark an item as found themselves.

What we stand for

  • Honesty Report only what you really lost or found.
  • Kindness Help someone get back something that matters to them.
  • Simplicity One account, one place to report, and one search box for the whole campus.

    FIRST TIME HERE?

    Welcome to Campus Lost & Found

    Quick answers to the questions new visitors ask most.

    What is Campus Lost & Found?

    A place for students to report things they lost on campus and things they found, so owners and finders can connect with each other.

    Do I need an account?

    Yes. Choose Create Account and enter your name, email, 8-digit registration number and a User ID. Click the password box and we will suggest a strong password. Then log in with your User ID and password.

    I lost something. What should I do?

    First search for it, because someone may already have found it. If it is not there, tap the three dots at the top left, choose Report Item, select Lost Item and add the name, where you lost it, the date and a short description.

    I found something. What should I do?

    Open the menu, choose Report Item and select Found Item. Say where you found it and describe it. The form then tells you where to submit the item, and you should hand it over there. Tip: keep one identifying detail to yourself, so you can check that the person claiming it is the real owner.

    How do I search for an item?

    Open the menu and choose Search. Type an item name, a place, or the word Lost or Found. Tap any card to see all of its details.

    What happens when my lost item is found?

    When someone reports a matching found item, your lost report is removed automatically and the found item's details are shown. If you get the item back yourself, open your lost report and tap Mark as found.

    How do I write a good report?

    Use a clear name such as "Black Samsung Galaxy phone", give the exact place and the correct date, and mention colour, brand, case or stickers. The more precise you are, the faster it gets matched.

    How do I change my profile picture or log out?

    Tap your round picture at the top right. You can see your details, pick a cartoon character, upload a photo, or log out.
